# Bellwether Coffee

> Bellwether Coffee gives cafés and coffee businesses everything they need to roast, brand, and sell great coffee — powered by the world's only electric, ventless, and automatic commercial coffee roaster. Roast fresh in-house, cut coffee costs by up to 50%, and serve coffee at peak freshness.

The Bellwether Shop Roaster (SCA Best New Product 2024) roasts 1.5 kg (3.3 lb) per batch and up to 400 lbs/week. It plugs into a 240V/30A outlet with no gas line, venting, or construction. US pricing is $14,900 (roaster) or $19,900 (bundle with the Continuous Roasting Kit), financing available. Most operators save $1,000–$5,000/month, with payback in as little as 6 months and up to 87% lower carbon than gas roasters. Beyond hardware it includes a Green Coffee Marketplace, a coffee team, a packaging and retail program, and ongoing service.
